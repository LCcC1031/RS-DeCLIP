# External projects

- [RemoteCLIP](https://github.com/ChenDelong1999/RemoteCLIP): pretrained remote-sensing dual encoder.
- [OpenAI CLIP](https://github.com/openai/CLIP): compatible model structure and tokenization.
- [MMRotate](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmrotate): Oriented R-CNN detector checkpoints.
- [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/) and [torchvision](https://github.com/pytorch/vision): tensor operations and ResNet implementation.
- [CLIP Benchmark](https://github.com/LAION-AI/CLIP_benchmark): retrieval recall utilities used by the supplied evaluator.

These projects are not vendored in this repository. Their code, checkpoints, and datasets remain subject to their respective licenses and usage conditions. The annotation files contain AI-polished training captions derived from their labeled source datasets; `dataset_source` and image filenames retain that provenance. Original image files and third-party paper PDFs are not included. The adapted Stage-1 checkpoint is distributed separately.
